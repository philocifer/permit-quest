## 🔧 What to Update Each Session

1. **Phase:** Current phase from roadmap (Phase 1, Phase 2, etc.)
2. **Next Task:** Specific task you want to work on
3. **Last Completed:** Brief note about previous session's progress
4. **What I need:** Specific request (implement, debug, review, etc.)

---

## 💡 Quick Tips

- ✅ **Always reference the roadmap** - I'll read it first for full context
- ✅ **Be specific** - Clear "Next Task" and "What I need" sections
- ✅ **Update after each session** - Keep status current
- ✅ **Keep it simple** - The template covers everything needed
